In that table, an ACT composite of 28 corresponds to an SAT total of about 1310 (range 1300–1320), so a 1300 SAT falls in the ACT 28 band. If you took the SAT and want to know what your score would look like on the ACT, or the other way around, you need the official concordance. It is the table colleges and counselors use to put the two scales side by side. You can also use our interactive [SAT to ACT score converter](/sat/sat-to-act-converter/) to calculate your exact score range instantly. This guide shows the part of the table most students need, explains how to read it, and lists what it cannot do. For a comparison of the tests themselves, see [SAT vs ACT](/guides/sat-vs-act/).

## Which table we used

We used the official "ACT/SAT concordance tables" published on ACT's website, specifically Table A2, which converts an ACT composite score to an SAT total score. ACT's page, which we read on November 2, 2026, says: "These tables compare scores but do not predict performance on the other test. The latest version was released in 2018 and is not updated annually."

ACT's page does not mention the 2025 changes to the ACT (the shorter test and the optional Science section), and we found no statement there on whether those changes affect the concordance. We also found no statement that they do not. If you rely on these tables for an important decision, check ACT's page for any update before you use them.

## How to convert an SAT score to ACT

To go from SAT to ACT, find your SAT total in the SAT range column and read the ACT composite in the same row.

- **1300 SAT to ACT:** The range 1300–1320 belongs to ACT 28, so a 1300 SAT corresponds to an ACT composite of 28.
- **1400 SAT to ACT:** The range 1390–1410 belongs to ACT 31, meaning an SAT 1400 converts to an ACT composite of 31.
- **1200 SAT to ACT:** The range 1200–1220 belongs to ACT 25.
- **1100 SAT to ACT:** The range 1100–1120 belongs to ACT 22.
- **1600 SAT to ACT:** The top SAT total of 1600 maps directly to the maximum ACT composite of 36 (range 1570–1600).

ACT also publishes the same mapping in the other direction (Table A1, SAT total to ACT composite), and it is the table to use when you want the exact entry for a specific SAT score. The ranges above are taken from Table A2, so they are a close guide, but check A1 for the official value of a particular score.

## Converting ACT scores to SAT (25, 28, 30, 32, 34 ACT to SAT)

When evaluating ACT practice scores against SAT benchmarks, Table A2 gives the corresponding SAT total and range:

- **25 ACT to SAT:** Converts to a single SAT score of 1210 (SAT range 1200–1220).
- **28 ACT to SAT:** Converts to an SAT total of 1310 (SAT range 1300–1320).
- **30 ACT to SAT:** Converts to an SAT total of 1370 (SAT range 1360–1380).
- **32 ACT to SAT:** Converts to an SAT total of 1430 (SAT range 1420–1440).
- **34 ACT to SAT:** Converts to an SAT total of 1500 (SAT range 1490–1520).

## How to read the numbers

A concordance matches score levels, not skills. A student who earns an ACT 28 and one who earns an SAT 1310 are said to be at about the same place in the score distribution, but they may have different strengths. That is why ACT's page says the tables do not predict performance on the other test.

Three practical consequences follow:

1. **Use ranges, not single numbers.** A score of 1305 and a score of 1315 both map to ACT 28, and the reverse conversion is also a range.
2. **Do not treat the conversion as a promise.** If you convert your SAT 1300 to an ACT 28, you have not shown that you would score a 28 on the ACT.
3. **Compare tests on the same day of practice.** The best way to know which test suits you is to take a timed practice test of each and compare them using this table.

## Section scores

ACT's page also contains separate tables for Math (B1 and B2) and for English plus Reading (C1 and C2). Use them if you want to compare a section rather than a total, for example to see whether your SAT Math score is in line with your ACT Math score.

## Where to find the official tables

ACT publishes the concordance on its website, and the same page links a guide to the concordance and the tables as a PDF and as a spreadsheet. Use those files if you need the complete table with every score.

## Frequently asked questions

### What ACT score is a 1300 on the SAT?

Using the ranges in ACT's Table A2, an SAT total of 1300 falls in the band for an ACT composite of 28 (SAT 1300–1320). For the exact entry, look up 1300 in the official SAT-to-ACT table.

### What is a 1400 SAT to ACT conversion?

An SAT total of 1400 falls into the 1390–1410 score band on Table A2, which maps directly to an ACT composite score of 31.

### What does a 1600 SAT score equal on the ACT?

A 1600 SAT total sits at the pinnacle of the ACT 36 composite range, which spans from 1570 to 1600.

### What is a 30 ACT to SAT conversion?

An ACT composite score of 30 corresponds to a single SAT concorded score of 1370, within an SAT range of 1360 to 1380.

### How often are the concordance tables updated?

ACT's page says the latest version was released in 2018 and that the tables are not updated every year.

### Does the 2025 ACT change affect the conversion?

ACT's concordance page does not say. We did not find a statement either way, so check the page for updates before you rely on the numbers.

### Why do the tables give a range of SAT scores for one ACT score?

The SAT scale has many more score points than the ACT's 1–36 scale, so several SAT totals map to the same ACT composite.
