<div class="key-takeaway"><p><strong>Quick answer:</strong> Both tests measure reading, listening, writing and speaking. The TOEFL iBT takes about two hours, adapts to your answers and reports scores on a 1–6 scale. IELTS Academic takes 2 hours 45 minutes, is scored in bands from 0 to 9, and has a face-to-face speaking test. Which one to take depends on what your university or program accepts and which format suits you, so check the requirement first.</p></div>

Choosing between TOEFL and IELTS is a common question for students who want to study in English. Both are widely used, and many institutions accept either. The tests differ in length, scoring and delivery, and the TOEFL format and scale changed in 2026, so older comparisons are out of date. This guide compares the two using the official pages from ETS and IELTS, which we read on November 2, 2026.

The TOEFL figures come from ETS's test content page and score guidance. The IELTS figures come from the IELTS Academic test page and the scoring page. IELTS General Training uses the same Listening and Speaking tests, but its Reading and Writing tests are different, and it is meant for migration or for training and study below degree level. This guide compares IELTS Academic, which is for undergraduate and postgraduate study or professional registration.

## How the two tests differ

**Adaptive or fixed.** ETS says the TOEFL iBT adapts, so the length and the number of items can vary. IELTS states a fixed time for each part of the test.

**Speaking.** The IELTS speaking test is a face-to-face conversation with an examiner on the computer-delivered version, and takes place in an online video call on IELTS Online. The TOEFL speaking section has two task types, Listen and Repeat and Take an Interview.

**Scoring.** On the new TOEFL scale, each section is scored 1–6 and the overall score is the average rounded to the nearest half band. IELTS gives each section a band from 0 to 9 and averages them in the same way, with specific rounding rules at .25 and .75. During a two-year transition after January 2026, ETS additionally reports a comparable overall score on the older 0–120 scale.

## Acceptance

Both tests are accepted by many universities, but acceptance is decided by each institution, not by the test provider, and the accepted score is set by each program. Do not rely on a general list. Instead:

1. Open the admissions page of your program and find the English-language requirement.
2. Note which tests are accepted, the minimum overall score and any section minimums.
3. Check whether the institution accepts home or online editions.
4. Check how long a score stays valid. IELTS recommends considering results valid for two years, and says organizations can choose a longer period.

If the program accepts both tests, choose the one in which you expect to score higher. A practice test of each is the best way to find out.

## How to choose

- **You prefer a shorter test and a computer-only experience:** the TOEFL iBT, if your institution accepts it.
- **You prefer a face-to-face speaking test with an examiner:** IELTS on computer at a test center.
- **You want to type your answers:** both offer computer delivery, and IELTS offers a paper writing option in selected countries.
- **You need a test for a visa that requires an approved test center:** check the visa rules, because IELTS says UKVI tests must be taken at an official test center.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is easier, TOEFL or IELTS?

Neither is easier in general. The tests use different formats, so people tend to score better on the one that suits their strengths. Take a practice test of each.

### Do all universities accept both tests?

No. Each institution sets its own policy and its own minimum scores, so check the requirement on your program's official page.

### Is IELTS General Training the same as IELTS Academic?

No. IELTS says that Listening and Speaking are the same, but Reading and Writing are different. Academic is for study and professional registration, and General Training is for migration or for training and study below degree level.

### How long are IELTS results valid?

IELTS recommends that results are considered valid for two years, and says organizations that accept IELTS can choose a longer period, so ask the organization you are applying to.
