Since January 21, 2026, score reports use a 1–6 scale in half-point steps instead of the older 0–120 scale. The test adapts, so the time and the number of items can vary. The TOEFL iBT is one of the English tests that universities accept as proof of language ability. ETS changed the test and its score scale in 2026, so older advice you find online may be out of date. This guide describes the current format as ETS publishes it and gives a preparation plan. Every fact about the test comes from ETS's pages, which we read on November 2, 2026, and which are listed at the end.

ETS notes that test time does not include directions, that you should allow approximately two hours, and that, because the test adapts, test time and items may vary. ETS also says that if you do not complete at least one Reading question, one Listening question, one Speaking task and one Writing task, your test will not be scored.

You can take the test on a computer at an authorized test center or as the TOEFL iBT Home Edition, on a computer at home monitored by a live human proctor. ETS states that writing assistance software is not permitted during the test.

## The score scale

For tests taken on or after January 21, 2026, ETS reports four section scores and an overall score on a 1–6 scale in half-point increments. The overall score is the average of the four sections, rounded to the nearest half band. For a two-year transition period after January 2026, reports also show a comparable overall score on the 0–120 scale. ETS says the new scale has a more intuitive alignment with the CEFR; our guide to the [CEFR levels](/guides/english-level-test-cefr-levels/) explains what those levels mean in practice.

ETS's pages give January 21, 2026 as the date of the new score scale. They describe the updated test as launching in 2026, and the pages we read do not give a separate date for the new task types, so we do not state one. If you took the test before January 21, 2026, your report uses the older scale. ETS's score pages explain how institutions read both formats.

## How the Writing section is built

According to ETS's 2026 test specifications, the twelve Writing items are ten Build a Sentence items, one Write an Email task and one Write for an Academic Discussion task. A Build a Sentence item is worth a maximum of 1 point, and each of the email and discussion responses is worth a maximum of 5 points. This tells you where to spend your practice: most items are about accurate sentence structure, and the two longer responses carry more points each.

## How to prepare

**1. Learn the task types.** Read ETS's test content page and the specification document so that no task surprises you on the day.

**2. Find your starting point.** Take an [English level test](/english/english-level-test/) and a full practice test from ETS. Note which section is weakest.

**3. Practice each skill separately.**

- *Reading:* read short everyday texts (notices, emails, schedules) and then academic passages. After each text, state the main idea in one sentence and list two details.
- *Writing:* practice reordering words into correct sentences, write short emails with a clear purpose and a polite tone, and write a reply to a discussion post that adds a new point with a reason.
- *Listening and speaking:* use ETS's official materials, since these skills need audio. Listen to varied English, and record yourself repeating and answering questions.

**4. Use timed practice.** Because the test adapts and has a time limit, practice finishing sections in the time allowed.

**5. Review every mistake.** Keep a list of the errors you make and the rule that fixes each one, and review it before every session.

## Test-day basics

Check ETS's current instructions for ID, the equipment you need and the rules for the Home Edition well before your date. Use the same device and environment for your last practice tests that you will use on the day.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the TOEFL iBT adaptive?

ETS's page says the test adapts, so time and the number of items may vary. It does not describe how the adaptation works, so check ETS's materials for the details.

### What is a good TOEFL score on the new scale?

It depends on the institution. Each university or program sets its own requirement, so look for the score it asks for on its official page.

### Can I use my phone or writing software during the test?

ETS says writing assistance software is not permitted during the test. Read the rules for your test format on ETS's site before you register.

### Do I have to take all four sections?

Yes. ETS says that if you do not complete at least one question or task in each section, your test will not be scored.
